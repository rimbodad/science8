2. Core Concepts

3.1 Ecosystems and Habitats

ConceptMust know
PopulationOne species in one area.
CommunityDifferent populations living in the same area.
EcosystemCommunity plus interactions with non-living factors.
HabitatThe place where an organism lives and thrives.
AdaptationsPhysical or behavioural features that help organisms survive in particular habitats.

3.2 Human Impact

ConceptMust know
Habitat destructionDeforestation and fragmentation reduce survival and biodiversity.
OverexploitationRemoving organisms too quickly can make populations collapse.
Invasive speciesCan prey on native species, spread disease or compete for resources.
PollutionLand, air and water pollution can harm organisms and food webs.
Persistent toxinsCan bioaccumulate in organisms and biomagnify along food chains.
High-value chapter idea: ecosystems are networks of interactions. Changing one species, one habitat condition or one pollutant can affect many other organisms.

3. Core Diagrams

Diagram A — Ecological organisation

One species in an area→Population→Different populations→Community
Community+Non-living factors→Ecosystem

Diagram B — Habitat → adaptation

Habitat conditions→Useful physical / behavioural features→Better survival

Diagram C — Human impact pathways

Deforestation / fragmentationHabitat loss → harder to find food/water/shelter → population decline → biodiversity loss.
OverexploitationToo many removed → reproduction cannot replace losses → endangered / extinct.
Invasive speciesPredation / disease / competition → native species decline → food web changes.
PollutionHarmful substances enter land/air/water → organisms harmed → ecosystem balance changes.

Diagram D — Fertiliser runoff

Fertiliser runoff→Extra nutrients→Algal bloom→Sunlight blocked→Underwater plants die

Diagram E — Bioaccumulation vs biomagnification

BioaccumulationToxin enters one organism → cannot be broken down/excreted → amount builds up over time.
BiomagnificationPersistent toxin moves through a food chain → higher-level consumers receive larger amounts.
Plankton

Do not confuse: bioaccumulation happens inside one organism over time; biomagnification happens across trophic levels in a food chain.

6. Final Mastery Checklist

3.1 Ecosystems & Habitats

  • I can distinguish population, community, ecosystem and habitat.
  • I can identify non-living factors.
  • I can classify major ecosystem types.
  • I can distinguish physical and behavioural adaptations.
  • I can explain adaptations using structure/function evidence.

3.2 Human Impact

  • I can explain deforestation, habitat fragmentation and overexploitation.
  • I can explain how invasive species disrupt ecosystems.
  • I can describe land, air and water pollution.
  • I can explain acid rain, algal blooms, microplastics and oil spills.
  • I can distinguish bioaccumulation and biomagnification.
  • I can use evidence to support/contradict a hypothesis and identify possible bias.
