Chapter 3.1 — Ecosystems and Habitats

Mục tiêu tự học: phân biệt population, community, ecosystem và habitat; nhận diện các loại ecosystem; hiểu living/non-living factors; và giải thích physical/behavioural adaptations.

1. Concept Map

Population
Organisms of one species in one area.
Community
All populations of different species in the same area.
Ecosystem
Interactions between organisms and non-living factors.
Habitat
The place where an organism lives and thrives.
Ecosystem types
Forest, grassland, desert, freshwater, saltwater.
Adaptations
Physical or behavioural features that help survival.

3. Core Knowledge

3.1 Population → Community → Ecosystem

One species in an area→Population→Different populations→Community
Community+non-living factors→Ecosystem
The SB names light, water and temperature as examples of non-living factors. Organisms also depend on one another for food and shelter.

3.2 Main ecosystem types

EcosystemCharacteristics from the SB
ForestMany trees and smaller plants; many animal species; lots of rainfall; shelter from wind; varied biodiversity.
GrasslandDifferent grasses, some shrubs and trees; open; lots of sunlight; affected by wind.
DesertLittle rainfall and much sunlight; communities often small because there are few producers.
FreshwaterOrganisms living in fresh water and on surrounding land.
SaltwaterOrganisms living in salty water; oceans contain a huge variety of life.

3.3 Habitat vs ecosystem

Ecosystem = whole interacting system.
Habitat = place where a particular organism lives and thrives.

SB example: a deer may live in a forest habitat, while a beetle may live in the bark of a tree.

4. Must-Know Relationships

One species→population
Different populations→community
Community + non-living factors→ecosystem
Habitat conditions→useful adaptations→better survival
Streamlined body→easier movement through water
Gripping tail / limbs→climbing and moving through trees
Important: a community contains living populations; an ecosystem also includes interaction with non-living factors.
